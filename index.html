    What do fitness, fatigue, form and the other numbers mean?
    Fitness
    How much training you've absorbed: your average daily training load over the last six weeks. It's the same idea as Strava's Fitness & Freshness chart (and TrainingPeaks' CTL), but built from Garmin's load, so the numbers won't match Strava's. Higher means you can handle more work. It rises slowly and falls slowly.
    Fatigue
    The same average over the last week only. It jumps after a big session or week and drops quickly with rest. It's roughly how tired you feel.
    Form
    Fitness minus fatigue, i.e. how fresh you are (Strava calls it Form or Freshness). Negative is normal while building: you're carrying some tiredness. Very negative for weeks means the load is outrunning your recovery. The taper is designed to make it positive on race day.
    Aerobic pace at 140 bpm
    What pace your steady runs would be if your heart rate sat at exactly 140. If the same heart rate buys a faster pace over months, your aerobic engine is growing. It's the running version of watts per heartbeat.
    Bike watts at 140 bpm
    The same for the bike: the power you'd hold at 140 bpm on a steady ride. Needs power and heart rate in the same ride.
    VO2 max
    Garmin's estimate of your maximum aerobic capacity, from pace against heart rate on every run. A change of 1 is real.
    Ramp
    How much the week's training load changed from the week before. More than about 10% a week, week after week, raises injury and illness risk.
